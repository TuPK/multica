# Local channels: Vietnam starting hypotheses

This list is a set of places to check, not a set of facts. It was written from general knowledge and has not been verified for current reach, fees, or rules. Before recommending any item, confirm with a search or with a member who sells in this market, and mark the result `observed` or `assumed`.

## B2C and small-business buyers
- Marketplaces: Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop, Tiki. Check seller fees, category rules, and ad costs.
- Social video: TikTok, Facebook Reels, YouTube Shorts. Check creator affiliate or KOC programs.
- Facebook: public groups and pages for the segment, Marketplace, Messenger ads. Group rules often forbid promotion; check before posting.
- Zalo: Official Account, Zalo groups, Zalo Ads. Strong for local and older audiences; verify.
- Google: Maps listings, local search, Google Ads. Relevant for anything with a physical place.
- Livestream selling and KOL/KOC partnerships: ask for audience demographics and past results before paying.

## B2B buyers
- LinkedIn: search, outreach, and groups.
- Industry associations, chambers of commerce, and trade shows in the segment's sector.
- Founder and operator communities on Facebook and Zalo; check which are active.
- Resellers, system integrators, and agencies that already serve the segment.
- Government and bank programs for small business support; verify eligibility and terms.

## Check before relying on any of the above
- Does it reach the named segment, or just a large audience?
- Is the platform's current policy compatible with the tactic?
- What did a comparable seller pay per lead or per customer, and where is that number from?
