# Channel catalog (19 traction channels)

The 19 channels from Gabriel Weinberg and Justin Mares' *Traction* (2014). The list is a prompt for coverage, not a ranking. Examples inside tactics are illustrative; the book predates today's platforms, so check what still works.

For each channel, propose a concrete tactic for the segment at hand. If a channel truly does not fit, write one line explaining why.

| # | Channel | Prompt for a tactic |
|---|---|---|
| 1 | Viral marketing | What makes one user bring another? Invites, shareable output, referral reward |
| 2 | Public relations (PR) | Which journalists or newsletters cover this segment's problem? What is the news? |
| 3 | Unconventional PR | A stunt, a study, a free giveaway, or a public dataset that earns coverage |
| 4 | Search engine marketing (SEM) | Which high-intent queries can be bought cheaply to test demand? |
| 5 | Social and display ads | Which platform lets us target the segment's job title, interest, or behaviour? |
| 6 | Offline ads | Local media, flyers, posters, radio, placement where the segment physically is |
| 7 | Search engine optimization (SEO) | Which queries does the segment search when the problem appears? |
| 8 | Content marketing | What would the segment bookmark or forward? Guides, templates, teardown posts |
| 9 | Email marketing | Which lists can we earn, borrow, or partner into? What is the first-email offer? |
| 10 | Engineering as marketing | A free tool, calculator, checker, or widget that solves a small part of the job |
| 11 | Targeting blogs | Which blogs, channels, or creators have the segment's attention? What do we offer them? |
| 12 | Business development (BD) | Which partner already has the customers and gains from our product? |
| 13 | Sales | Who can we call or message directly, and what is the opening question? |
| 14 | Affiliate programs | Who is paid per customer they send? Where are the existing affiliates? |
| 15 | Existing platforms | Which marketplace, app store, or integration directory already holds the segment? |
| 16 | Trade shows | Which show does the segment attend? What is the cheapest way to be present? |
| 17 | Offline events | Host or join a meetup, workshop, or class for the segment |
| 18 | Speaking engagements | Which stages, podcasts, or webinars reach the segment? What talk would they want? |
| 19 | Community building | Which group do we start or join? What do members get that they can't elsewhere? |
