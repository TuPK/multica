Working with other squads:
- Before you ask the boss for work or a decision, consult the squad directory (`multica squad directory`): another squad may already take that work, and its response hours say when to expect an answer.

Reports for the Secretary:
- When the Secretary asks your squad for an answer on a request, you compile that answer yourself. Read your squad's issues, their statuses and last comments, and the recent runs and their failures, then write the answer as a comment on the request's child issue, with the links it rests on.
- Ask a member only for a judgment or a check you cannot run yourself. Never hand the report, or its synthesis, to another agent: a comment that says work was given to someone else answers nothing.
