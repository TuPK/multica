# Market: Vietnam (mkt-market-vn)

Status: beta. The legal section needs review by the legal owner before this skill is treated as stable.

## 1. Language and address

- Write the public text in Vietnamese with full diacritics. Keep internal notes in the language of the brief.
- Default address: neutral and polite. Use the brand's own forms of address when the voice document sets them.
- Product names and English terms stay in English when the market uses them that way.

## 2. Channels, and what changes in the method advice

| Channel | Use it for | What changes compared with the English method skill |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Facebook and Instagram | Reach, retargeting, fanpage content | Short hooks, video first, comments are part of the funnel |
| Zalo (Official Account, groups) | Retention, orders, support | Message-based follow-up; no email assumption |
| TikTok, YouTube Shorts | Discovery, short video | Creator-led content is the norm; scripts run under 30 seconds for the first hook |
| Google Search | Intent, high-value terms | Vietnamese and English queries both appear; check both |
| Marketplaces (Shopee, Lazada, TikTok Shop) | Purchase | Price, ratings and delivery speed decide; landing-page advice moves to the listing |

Use the channel list the brief or the knowledge base gives. Do not add a channel the business does not run.

## 3. Search and AI search in Vietnamese

- Research keywords with and without diacritics, because people type both.
- Check the Vietnamese SERP yourself on the web; do not assume the English results carry over.
- For AI answers, the question is whether the product is named when a person asks in Vietnamese. Test it with several phrasings and record what you saw with a date.

## 4. Payment and trust

- Cash on delivery, e-wallets and bank transfer are common. Say which ones the business offers, and never assume card payment.
- Trust comes from delivery and return terms, reviews the buyer can see, and a named seller. Put these above the fold on a landing page before the discount.

## 5. Seasons

Check the calendar for Tết Nguyên Đán (plan at least six weeks ahead, since shops close and deliveries slow), the mid-year and year-end sales events, and the school year. If the workspace has the seasonal calendar skill, use it; otherwise write `MISSING: seasonal calendar`.

## 6. Adjustments to the method skills

- Scarcity, urgency and social proof lines ("only 3 left", "1,000 buyers") are factual claims. Each one needs a source and a date, and goes through the claim gate before it is public.
- Discount wording is a promotion. Check the promotion rules in section 8 before writing it.
- Measurement: most sales here close off-site. Ask which of the conversions the business can really record (order system, Zalo, call) and write `MISSING` for the rest.

## 7. Evidence

Every number about this market carries its source, its year and the date you read it. A number you cannot source is written as `[cần bổ sung: nguồn]`, not stated from memory.
