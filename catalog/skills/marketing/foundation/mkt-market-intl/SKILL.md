# Market: international (mkt-market-intl)

Status: beta. The regional rules need review by the legal owner before this skill is treated as stable. Split this skill by region when a second region is in scope (for example `mkt-market-eu`).

## 1. Language and locale

- Write the public text in the language of the brief. Confirm the locale (spelling, currency, date format) with the brief before drafting.
- Do not translate idioms literally. Adapt the sentence, then check it with the voice document.

## 2. Channels and norms

- Search and social channels differ by region. Name the channels the brief lists, and check which ones the audience in that region actually uses before recommending a new one.
- Marketplaces and app stores have their own policies on claims, reviews and incentives. Read the current policy of each listed one on the web and record the date.

## 3. Search and AI search

- Research keywords in the target language, not by translating the English list.
- For AI answers, test the question in the target language and record which sources are named, with a date.

## 4. Consent and privacy

Email, SMS and tracking consent differ by region and by the kind of message. Each rule below needs a current official source and a read date before it is applied:

- Commercial email and marketing messages: `[cần bổ sung: nguồn và ngày đọc, theo khu vực]`
- Cookies and tracking on the site: `[cần bổ sung: nguồn và ngày đọc, theo khu vực]`
- Personal data in forms, lead magnets and referrals: `[cần bổ sung: nguồn và ngày đọc, theo khu vực]`

Default to the stricter reading when the sources disagree, and say so in the handover list.

## 5. Ads, endorsements and AI content

- Ad platforms change their policies often. Read the current policy of each platform in the plan on the web and record its date.
- Endorsements and reviews: `[cần bổ sung: nguồn và ngày đọc, theo khu vực]`.
- Labels for AI-generated or synthetic content: `[cần bổ sung: nguồn và ngày đọc, theo khu vực]`.

## 6. Adjustments to the method skills

- Claims about results must name a source, a date and a sample. Without them the claim is a draft and goes through the claim gate.
- Urgency and scarcity are checked against the actual inventory or the actual deadline, not written for effect.

## 7. Evidence

Every figure about a market carries its source, its year and the date you read it. A figure you cannot source is written as `[cần bổ sung: nguồn]`.

## 8. Final check

The final legal conclusion belongs to the person responsible for legal review. Mark any draft that touches section 4 or 5 as `cần soát pháp lý` in the handover list.
