# Secretary leader questions: examples

## Answered from the boss's words

The Build lead asks on a directive: "Does the boss still want the 28 November date, or
has it moved?"

Reply on the thread, quoting the boss:

> The boss said in chat on 6 November: "Go with 28 November for checkout." Nothing
> since has moved it. If the date changes, the boss will say so in chat.

## Routed to another leader

The Release lead asks: "Has the Finance squad signed off the tax rule budget?"

The question is Finance's work. File a child issue for the Finance lead as
`secretary-ask` describes, then tell the Release lead on the thread:

> I have asked the Finance lead for the tax rule budget status. The answer will come
> back on this thread, with its date.

## Brought to the boss

The Platform lead asks: "Shall we pause the migration for the Black Friday week?"

This is a decision for the boss. Bring it in chat with a recommendation:

> Platform asks whether to pause the migration for Black Friday week. Recommendation:
> pause from 24 to 30 November; the migration has no dependency on the checkout
> launch, and the risk of an outage in that week is the larger one. Your decision.
