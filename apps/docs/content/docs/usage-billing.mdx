Besides seat plans (see [Subscriptions](/subscriptions)), a workspace can be on one of two other kinds of plan. Amounts are in VND.

## The unlimited plan

The unlimited plan sets none of the counted limits: issues, automation runs, agents, runtimes, and storage are all off. It is still priced per seat, and in the default catalogue it is open to both account types.

"Unlimited" is not infinite. The API request rate stays under a platform-wide ceiling, and audit log retention still has a limit.

## The pay-as-you-go plan

A pay-as-you-go plan has no seats and no payment up front. You use the workspace, and the invoice comes after the month.

To start it, choose the plan in **Settings → Billing**. It starts at once, with an order of 0. The billing profile must be filled in first:

- An individual account needs a legal name and an invoice email.
- A business account needs all four fields. See [Subscriptions](/subscriptions#individual-and-business-accounts).

A workspace on a paid seat plan cannot switch to a pay-as-you-go plan in this release.

Usage is counted per UTC day on three meters:

| Meter | What is counted |
|---|---|
| Tasks run | Agent runs that completed in the day. Failed runs are not counted. |
| Storage | Bytes of attachments at the end of the day. The monthly price is shared over the days of the month. |
| Members | Members at the end of the day. The monthly price is shared over the days of the month. |

Each day is priced at the plan's rate and kept as a line with the price it was rated at, so a later price change does not alter past days. A suspended workspace is rated at zero. The **Usage this period** card shows the quantity, rate, and amount of each meter for the current month.

## Usage invoices

At the start of each UTC month, the days of the previous month become one usage invoice. It adds VAT at the rate in `BILLING_VAT_RATE` (10% when it is not set). It is a bank-transfer order like any other, and it does not expire. **View details** under **Invoices** lists the daily lines.

- **Minimum amount.** A monthly invoice below the minimum is not issued, and its lines carry over to the next month. The default minimum is 10,000 VND, set by `PLATFORM_USAGE_MIN_INVOICE`. The final invoice, issued when a workspace leaves a pay-as-you-go plan, has no minimum.
- **Due date.** An invoice that stays unpaid for 7 days (`PLATFORM_USAGE_DUE_DAYS`) moves the subscription to `past_due`.
- **Unpaid invoice.** While a usage invoice is unpaid, buying or changing to a paid plan is refused. A banner links to the invoice. Changing to the free plan is still allowed.

## Credit limit

A pay-as-you-go plan can set a credit limit for each account type. It is the amount of usage that has accrued and not yet been invoiced. A plan with no limit for your account type has none.

- At **80%**, the card shows a warning, and owners and admins get one email in that month.
- At **100%**, an interim invoice for all uninvoiced usage is issued at once, with no minimum.

The limit never blocks usage.

## Business discount

A plan can give business accounts a percentage discount on usage. It applies only to `business` accounts. By default it applies only when staff have verified the billing profile. If your administrator sets `PLATFORM_BUSINESS_DISCOUNT_REQUIRES_VERIFICATION` to `false`, every business account gets it. Each meter and invoice line shows the discount it was rated with. Changing the legal name or tax code removes the verification.

<Callout type="info">
The discount is rounded once per daily line, not once per invoice.
</Callout>
