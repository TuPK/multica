## Chatting with an agent

Chat fits work that has not become a clear issue: sorting out ideas, asking a question that belongs to no issue, or discussing an approach. A chat is a one-to-one conversation with an agent, outside the issue board. Other members cannot see it. Conclusions the team needs belong in an issue, a project description or a knowledge document.

A chat is not tied to an issue, so the agent does not automatically read any issue's description or comments. You can attach a project to a conversation to give the agent context.

Each message creates a run, and the reply returns to the conversation. Later messages continue the same tool session, so you do not need to repeat earlier content.

**Floating chat**: from any page, the round button in the bottom-right corner opens a small window. Press `Cmd/Ctrl+J` to open or close it; opening it places the cursor in the input.

**Queue**: while an agent is replying, new messages join a queue. **Steer** on a queued message stops the current reply first and then handles this one. Content already returned is kept. If the agent had produced nothing yet, your message returns to the input, attachments included, so you can edit it.

To stop midway, use the stop button next to the input.

## The Secretary

The Secretary is one agent for the whole workspace. It reads what your squads did and writes one brief for you each day: what needs your decision, what is moving, and what is stuck. You do not receive a report from each squad. Squads work through issues, projects and proposals; the Secretary pulls the facts it needs, so squads spend no runs on status reports.

### What the Secretary does

- Asks the leaders who own each part of a question, reads their answers and challenges the thin ones. Each request has a limit on challenges.
- Gives you one assessment: the answer first, the decisions you need to make, the risks ranked with an owner each, what leaders disagreed on, and what it did not check.
- Passes your decisions to the leader who will carry them out, as a **directive**. A directive can only be created from a chat message sent by an owner or admin, and your words are stored exactly as written.

The Secretary does not approve proposals or launch gates. Those keep their Approve and Reject buttons for you. It cannot change an issue's status or assignee, delete anything, or publish knowledge unless a publishing policy allows it.

### The daily brief

Each morning the Secretary publishes a brief from the company's digest: a short headline, the decisions waiting for you, progress by floor, goals that changed, budgets, pipeline, people reviews, risks and notes. It takes about two minutes to read. If the Secretary's machine was asleep at eight o'clock, the brief is caught up later the same day.

The notification **Brief ready** is the only routine notification from the Secretary. Rate each brief as useful or not useful, with a reason tag (too long, missing something, wrong, too vague, not for me). The Secretary reads your recent ratings and adjusts.

### Briefs in chat apps

You can receive the brief in Slack, Telegram or Lark after linking your account. The message is short: the headline, the number of decisions waiting, the top three with buttons to **Approve**, **Reject** or **Snooze 7 days**, and a link to the full brief. Only owners and admins can decide from a chat app. You can set quiet hours when nothing is sent.

In a chat with the Secretary, `/brief` shows the latest brief and `/decisions` lists what is waiting for you.
