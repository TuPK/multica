# Secretary brief: examples and vocabulary

## Pipeline vocabulary

- An initiative is one venture moving through the company, named INI-<seq>. It is the
  unit the boss thinks in. A project is a body of work; an issue is a task.
- The stages in order are Validate, Define, Plan, Build, Launch, Operate, and Learn.
  Each stage has an owning squad and required artifacts: knowledge documents of a given
  kind.
- A stage's exit is one of three modes: auto (advances when its required artifacts are
  published), attest (the owning squad attests, then it advances), or gate (the boss
  decides on a stage gate proposal).
- Initiative statuses are active, shelved (paused and hidden from the active board),
  operating (launched, under its 30 and 90 day reviews), and closed.
- A stalled stage is one past its SLA days. The digest flags it. It is a flag, not a move.

## A quiet day

> ## Headline
> Nothing needs you today.
>
> ## Progress
> Customer Voice: no news. Growth: one launch closed, see the link.
>
> ## FYI
> 2 drafts await publishing.

The headline is one line. Sections with nothing in them are left out, except Progress,
which keeps one line per floor.

## A decision day

> ## Headline
> The checkout launch needs your decision by Thursday. Two items, one blocker.
>
> ## Needs your decision
> D1. Checkout launch: 14 November (40 percent clean-launch chance) or 28 November (on
> track). Recommendation: 28 November. Owner: you. Assessment: [link].
>
> ## Blockers and risks
> The payment provider has not answered the tax-rule question. Proposed way out: ask
> the Finance lead for a date today.

Five items at most under Needs your decision. The sixth and later are counted, not
listed: "and 3 more, see the digest."

## Feedback that changes the next brief

Three entries carry `too_long`. The Secretary records: "Keep briefs under a page; put
the decision in the first three lines." The next brief follows that, and the
preference is saved to memory with the date.

One entry says "not useful" with no tag and no note. It is not a preference. Nothing
changes.
