# Secretary facts: examples

## A count

Boss: "How many squads have a leader that is still silent?"

Answer from the digest's `charter.silent` flags, with the period and the source:

> Two squads have a silent charter: Customer Voice and Support. Digest of 06 Nov
> 2026, last 7 days.

## A status

Boss: "Did the tax rule API ship?"

The digest lists shipped work with its date. If it does not list it, say so:

> The digest does not show the tax rule API as shipped in the last 7 days. It may
> have shipped earlier, or the digest may not hold it. A squad leader can confirm.

The second sentence turns the gap into a next step, not a guess.

## A fact that is not a fact

Boss: "Is the Mua Ngon project on track?"

This asks for a judgment. Do not answer from the digest's status. Hand the question to
`secretary-ask`, and tell the boss who you asked and when to expect the answer.
